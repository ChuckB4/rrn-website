Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 17, 2026

Please note: This policy is provided for transparency and is not legal advice. Refined Recovery Network LLC recommends review by a licensed attorney before relying on it. Questions: info@joinrrn.com.

Refined Recovery Network LLC (“RRN,” “we,” “us”) operates joinrrn.com and the Hope Wall as a faith-based peer recovery ministry. We respect your privacy and encourage anonymous sharing. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What We Collect

  • Information you choose to share. Hope Wall submissions, contact and beta forms, and similar fields. You may share a first name or nickname and your state if you wish; a last name and exact address are never required and never displayed.
  • Optional contact details. An email address only if you provide one so our team can follow up privately.
  • Technical metadata. Like most websites, our hosting and any analytics may log technical information such as IP address, browser type, device, and pages visited. Even anonymous submissions may carry such metadata.

Please do not post personally identifiable medical information, and do not identify or make accusations against other people or organizations.

Not a HIPAA-Covered Entity

RRN is a faith-based recovery ministry and is generally not a HIPAA-covered entity. We do not promise HIPAA compliance. We do take reasonable steps to protect the information you share and to keep public submissions anonymous.

Cookies & Analytics

We may use basic cookies and analytics tools to understand how the site is used and to improve it. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Moderation & AI Review

Hope Wall submissions are reviewed before they appear. Review may include automated (AI) screening and human moderation to remove names of third parties, contact details, specific allegations, and content that violates these policies.

How We Use Information

  • To review, moderate, and (where you consent) publish anonymized stories.
  • To respond to you if you request follow-up.
  • To operate, secure, and improve our services.

Sharing & Law Enforcement

We do not sell your information. We may disclose information if required by law, valid legal process, or to protect the safety of any person.

Data Retention & Deletion

We retain submissions and related metadata only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you submitted, email info@joinrrn.com.

Children

The Hope Wall and story submissions are for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect publicly displayed stories from minors. If you believe a minor has submitted information, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “last updated” date will change accordingly.
