Not My Will, But Thine

Making the decision is not a feeling you wait for. It is a turn of the will. Step 3 is the moment you stop negotiating with God over the steering wheel and actually hand it over — not because you feel strong, but because self-will has already proven where it drives.

This is the hardest thing a person in recovery does, and the most freeing. The same prayer Jesus prayed in His darkest hour is the one that sets a bound life loose: not my will, but Thine. It is short. It is honest. And it can be prayed before a single circumstance has changed.

You are not surrendering to a hard master. You are surrendering to the One who has wanted to carry this for you the whole time.

“Nevertheless not my will, but thine, be done.”— Luke 22:42

You don't have to walk this out alone.
