Confess, and Be Healed

Something stays in power as long as it stays a secret. Step 5 — admitting to God, to yourself, and to one trusted person the exact nature of what you have carried — is where the secret loses its weight.

The Scripture ties confession directly to healing. Not exposure for the sake of shame, but bringing the hidden thing into the open so it can finally be set down. The one who hears it is not your judge; they are a witness that the truth has been told and the load is no longer yours to carry alone.

Walk it out faithfully. What you confess in the light cannot keep ruling you from the dark.

“Confess your faults one to another, and pray one for another, that ye may be healed.”— James 5:16

You don't have to walk this out alone.
