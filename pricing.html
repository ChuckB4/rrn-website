Branded / Dedicated Deployment

Branded RRN Deployment

$1,500–$3,000 one-time setup

A separately configured, separately hosted RRN deployment for your ministry — your name, your terminology, your own instance.

Included: organization name & terminology configuration, contact-information branding, a dedicated separately-deployed application instance.

Not yet available in any deployment: custom domain, logo upload, live self-service branding changes.

Ongoing hosting/maintenance, if RRN continues to host your instance: $50–$150/month.

One-time implementation and deployment fee. Subscription required. This fee covers configuring and deploying your dedicated instance — it does not include the underlying RRN platform itself. Your instance still runs on a Ministry, Network, Regional, or Enterprise plan (above), billed separately and continuously.

Is this right for you?

This is a configured, dedicated deployment delivered by our team — not a self-service white-label toggle inside a shared platform. If your organization wants its own separately branded and hosted copy of RRN, this is the path.

Live, self-service organization branding inside one shared platform is on our roadmap — see Coming Soon below.
