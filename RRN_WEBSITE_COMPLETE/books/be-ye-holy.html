About the Book

“Be ye holy; for I am holy.” (1 Peter 1:16, KJV)

A book on inner holiness by Tina Renee Bradley, written to help the reader understand how to live a Christlike life set apart unto God.

Before you turn the first page

Holiness is not a list of rules. It is a love that changes you from the inside out.

In a world that calls compromise “freedom,” Tina Renee Bradley writes a quiet, steady call back to something deeper — a life set apart, not by striving, but by surrender. “Be Ye Holy” walks through what it truly means to be made clean, to be kept, and to belong wholly to God.

This is for the believer who wants more than forgiveness — who wants to be transformed. Who has tasted grace and now hungers for a life that looks like the One who saved it.

“Be ye holy; for I am holy.” It is not a burden. It is an invitation. Open the first page and answer it.
